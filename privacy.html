Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

The short version: Enclyra runs on your Mac. Your files, your chats and your memory stay on your Mac. We receive data only when you send a report or feedback, or when a step fails and you chose Always send. Before a report leaves your Mac, Enclyra takes out file names, folder names and file contents. That filter works in most cases in our testing, but it is not perfect. We use what we receive to improve Enclyra, including to train our AI.

1. Who we are

Enclyra is made by EnclyraLabs ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the Enclyra app for macOS ("the app") does with your data.

2. What stays on your Mac

Everything below is stored only on your Mac and is never sent to us:

  • Your files and folders, their contents, and everything the app does with them.
  • Your conversations with the assistant.
  • Enclyra's memory: what it learns about you, your habits and your preferences.
  • The file index the app builds so it can find your files fast.
  • Your settings and personal details you enter.
  • A working log of the actions Enclyra ran: your requests, the steps, the files they used, and any errors. It records actions only — never Enclyra's memory or your chats. Only the newest ~8 MB is kept; older lines are deleted automatically. You can delete it any time.
  • A network log: each time Enclyra went online, and where to.

The AI models run on your Mac (through Ollama / llama.cpp). Your requests are not sent to a cloud AI.

App data is kept in ~/Library/Application Support/EnclyraAI. You can delete that folder at any time to remove it.

3. What we receive, and when

We receive data in only these cases:

  • You press Send on a failure report, on the bug button of a finished plan, or on feedback.
  • A step fails and you chose "Always send". Then the report is sent automatically, without showing it to you first. You can change this at any time (see section 8).

Nothing else is ever sent to us.

a) Failure reports and the bug button. A report contains:

  • the app version, your macOS version and your chip type (for example "macOS 15.1 arm64");
  • the name of the step that failed, and its error message;
  • what you asked (up to 600 characters);
  • the app's technical log for that one request (up to 6,000 characters), action steps only: which steps ran, in what order, and what went wrong. Lines about Enclyra's memory, your chats or what it learned about you are never included;
  • for the bug button: the list of steps the plan ran.

b) Feedback (the Report button). The text you type, plus the app version and your macOS version. What you type is sent exactly as you wrote it. Please don't put personal details in it.

The filter. Before a failure report or bug report is sent, Enclyra removes:

  • file contents: text the app read, wrote, summarised, copied or saw on your screen is taken out of the log, and step results that could repeat it are hidden;
  • file and folder names, replaced by stand-ins like FILE_1.pdf and FOLDER_1;
  • email addresses, links and phone numbers, replaced by EMAIL_1, URL_1, PHONE_1;
  • your Mac user name.

Private data check. On top of the filter, Enclyra checks every report for things like a PIN, a password, an OTP, a card number, an ID number (such as Aadhaar, PAN or SSN), a bank account number or a secret key. If it finds one, the report is not sent, not even with "Always send". It is kept on your Mac only, under Settings → Storage & privacy → Held reports, where you can read it, send it yourself, or delete it. If feedback you typed looks private, Enclyra asks before sending it.

This filter and check work in most cases in our lab testing, but they are not perfect. Some personal detail could still get through, especially in what you typed. When you choose Send, the dialog shows you exactly what will be sent first. Please look at it. If you don't want to take any risk, choose Never send.

We never receive: your files, your memory, your chat history, your contacts, your email, your calendar, your passwords, or anything from a report you did not send or allow.

4. Your consent, and how we use the data

We collect reports only because you choose to send them (by pressing Send, or by choosing Always send). That choice is your consent.

By sending a report or feedback, you give EnclyraLabs a free, worldwide, permanent right to keep, copy, study, change and use it. We may use it in any way that helps us build and improve Enclyra and our other products, including:

  • finding and fixing bugs;
  • testing and improving how the app plans and acts;
  • training and improving our own AI systems and models;
  • studying how the app is used, in total.

Reports may be read by people on our team, not only by machines.

We do not sell reports, and we do not share them with advertisers.

5. How we store reports

  • Reports are stored in a database run for us by our hosting providers, Vercel and Upstash. Their servers may be outside your country, including in the United States. By sending a report, you agree to that.
  • We may also keep copies of reports on our own storage.
  • We do not store your IP address. To stop spam, our server makes a short scrambled code (a hash) from the IP address. That code is deleted after 2 minutes.
  • Reports have no account, name or email attached. We cannot tell who sent a report.
  • We may keep reports for as long as we need them to improve Enclyra.
  • We protect the database with access keys. But no system on the internet is 100% secure, and we can't promise that the data will never be accessed without permission.

Because reports are anonymous, we usually can't find a specific report to delete it. If you sent something by mistake, contact us with the time you sent it and what it said, and we will try.

6. When we may share data

We don't share reports with anyone, except:

  • our hosting providers, who store them for us (see section 5);
  • if the law, a court or a government authority requires it;
  • if EnclyraLabs or Enclyra is ever merged, sold or transferred. The reports may then go to the new owner, who must follow this policy.

7. When the app goes online

The app works offline by default. It connects to the internet only in these cases:

  • Update check: when the app starts (if updates are on), it checks our website for a new version. None of your data is sent.
  • "Am I online?" check: a quick look-up so the app can honestly say when you are offline. No data.
  • When you ask for something online: web search, news, pictures, videos, downloads, opening websites, price watch, currency rates, your public IP address, or an internet speed test. The search words, link or product you ask for go to that service (for example DuckDuckGo, Google News, YouTube, Amazon, Keepa, Wikipedia, ipify, Cloudflare). Those services have their own privacy policies.
  • Commands you approve: installs (pip, npm, brew), git and model downloads reach the internet, the same as if you ran them yourself.
  • Sending reports or feedback, as described above.

The app writes every one of these connections in a network log on your Mac (Settings → Storage & privacy → Open the network log), so you can check for yourself.

8. Your choices

  • Change your report choice at any time: Settings → Storage & privacy → Failure reports → Ask me each time / Always send / Never send.
  • If you don't press Send and don't choose Always send, nothing reaches us.
  • Turn off update checks in Settings → General → Updates.
  • Delete ~/Library/Application Support/EnclyraAI to remove the app's data from your Mac.

Changing your choice stops future reports. It does not remove reports you already sent.

9. Permissions on your Mac

macOS may ask you to allow things like Files and Folders, Screen Recording (for reading text on your screen), Accessibility, Calendar, Reminders, Notes or Music. These permissions let the app do what you ask on your own Mac. Data the app reads through them stays on your Mac. You can turn them off at any time in System Settings → Privacy & Security.

10. Children

Enclyra is not made for children under 13. We don't knowingly collect data from children. If you are under the age where your country requires a parent's consent, please use Enclyra only with that consent.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top and post the new version on this page. If the change is important, we will also say so in the app or on our website. Reports you sent before a change stay under the policy you sent them under, unless you agree otherwise.
